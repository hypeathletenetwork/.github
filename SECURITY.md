# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you have found a security problem, please email **privacy@hypeapp.world**.
Do not open a public issue or discuss it in public.

Tell us what you found, where, how to reproduce it, and what you think the
impact is. We will review every report and tell you what we decide.

## Please

- Act in good faith, and test only with your own accounts.
- Do not access, change or keep anyone else's data. Our users include minors.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it.

## If you find a credential

If you find a key, token or password that should not be public, report it to
the same address and do not use it.
